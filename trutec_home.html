Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most from UAE enterprises exploring IT partnerships.

IT Infrastructure
What server and storage brands do you supply and support?+

We are authorised partners for Dell Technologies, HPE, Cisco, Pure Storage, and NetApp — among others. We procure exclusively through authorised distribution channels, ensuring genuine products, valid manufacturer warranties, and full UAE in-country support. We do not source grey-market hardware.

Can you deploy servers in air-gapped or classified environments?+

Yes. Our team has hands-on experience deploying complex IT infrastructure — including multi-node GPU clusters and large-scale storage — in fully air-gapped, dark-site, and classified environments. We understand the additional logistics, security protocols, and documentation requirements these projects demand.

How long does a typical server or storage deployment take?+

Timelines vary by project scale. A straightforward rack server deployment with standard configuration can be completed in 3–5 business days from procurement. Complex multi-node environments — including cabling, firmware, OS configuration, and integration — typically take 2–4 weeks. We provide a detailed project schedule at proposal stage.

Do you offer Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) for hardware?+

Yes. We provide structured AMC packages covering preventive maintenance, firmware and patch management, break-fix support, loaner device provision, and SLA-backed response times (4-hour, next business day, or custom). AMCs can be tailored to cover specific equipment or your entire IT estate.

Cyber Security
What is the difference between EDR and XDR?+

EDR (Endpoint Detection and Response) focuses specifically on protecting endpoints — laptops, desktops, and servers — using behavioural analysis, threat hunting, and MITRE ATT&CK-aligned detection. XDR (Extended Detection and Response) goes further by correlating telemetry across endpoints, the network, email, identity, and cloud — giving your security team a unified view of threats across the entire environment. We assess your environment and recommend the right level of coverage.

Is TruTecME familiar with UAE PDPL and DIFC data protection requirements?+

Yes. We have deep familiarity with the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021), the DIFC Data Protection Law No. 5 of 2020 (as amended by Law No. 1 of 2025 effective July 2025), ADGM Data Protection Regulations 2021, and GDPR. We help organisations map their data flows, identify compliance gaps, and implement both technical controls and procedural frameworks to meet their obligations.

How quickly can you respond to a cybersecurity incident?+

Response times depend on your contracted SLA. We offer managed support with 1-hour, 4-hour, and next-business-day response options. For clients with active SOC managed services, we provide continuous monitoring with automated alerting — meaning suspicious activity is flagged and investigated around the clock, not just during business hours.

Digital Transformation & ERP
What is ERPNext and how does it compare to SAP or Microsoft Dynamics?+

ERPNext is a fully open-source ERP platform built on the Frappe framework. It covers accounting, HR, payroll, inventory, manufacturing, CRM, and project management in one system. Unlike SAP or Dynamics, ERPNext has no per-user licensing fees — you pay only for implementation, customisation, and hosting. It's an excellent choice for UAE SMEs and mid-market companies that need enterprise-grade functionality without six-figure annual licence costs. We implement, customise, and host ERPNext with full UAE VAT compliance, WPS payroll, and Arabic language support.

How long does an ERP implementation take?+

A standard ERPNext implementation for a mid-sized company (50–200 users) typically takes 8–16 weeks from kickoff to go-live, depending on the number of modules, data migration complexity, and custom development required. SAP S/4HANA and Microsoft Dynamics 365 implementations for larger organisations typically run 4–9 months. We provide a phased project plan with clear milestones at proposal stage.

What is a Digital Business Card and how does the Apple Wallet integration work?+

Our Digital Business Card solution replaces printed cards with an NFC-enabled physical card or a QR code that instantly shares your professional profile. When someone taps your card or scans your QR code, they see your name, role, company, phone, email, and links — and with one tap they can add it to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet as a pass, or save it directly to their phone contacts. Your details are managed through an online dashboard, so any changes you make (new phone number, new title) are reflected on everyone's saved cards instantly.
